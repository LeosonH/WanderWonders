# Wander Wonders V1 privacy and publication readiness

Status: publication checklist opened; public URLs and legal text are owner-controlled inputs.

## Publication checklist

- [ ] Privacy Policy is published at the owner-approved final URL
- [ ] Support page is published at the owner-approved final URL
- [ ] Signed-out browser check returns HTTP 200 for both pages
- [ ] Final domains match TestFlight metadata and in-app Settings links
- [ ] Feedback contact is monitored
- [ ] Account-deletion guidance covers successful deletion and manual Apple-revocation guidance
- [ ] Location explanation says “near a park” and does not claim “inside the park”
- [ ] Health/Motion explanation states that raw samples, sources, devices, routes, and coordinates are not retained
- [ ] Identity, notification, offline-cache, and pending-deletion behavior is documented accurately
- [ ] Owner reviews the generated `PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy` and `docs/privacy-data-map.md` after implementation

## Not yet verified

No URL, legal statement, provider response, or data-practice claim is treated as final until the owner supplies it and the implementation-backed publication check passes.
